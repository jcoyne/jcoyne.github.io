Thanksgiving plan

Dinner at 6:00 Thursday, using one oven. Checkboxes are saved in this browser.

Notes

  • Turkey breast timing: about 2 hours in total (3:15 at 375°, then 400° from 4:05). Go by the thermometer, not the clock: start checking at 4:45, then every 10 minutes, in the thickest part without touching bone. If it's boneless, it will cook faster.
  • If the breast is slow: if it isn't at 155° by about 5:30, put the thighs in at 400° next to it, and raise the oven to 500° once the breast comes out.
  • Browning: if the breast skin gets too dark before the inside is done, tent the top loosely with foil.
  • If the confit runs late: the squash still finishes in time as long as it goes in by about 1:40, before the oven drops to 325° at 2:35.
  • If you sleep past 6:30: the cheese bread needs 8–12 hours to thaw, so push its bake later, and every 15 minutes of delay pushes the turkey back about 15 minutes.
  • After dinner: strain the confit fat and save it to reuse.
